**Day 2 – Recruitment Lifecycle Understanding 
The objective of Day 2 was to understand the initial stages of the IT recruitment lifecycle, from identifying manpower requirements to conducting recruiter screening.

**Recruitment Flow

Manpower Requirement  
↓  
Job Requisition  
↓  
Job Description (JD)  
↓  
Candidate Sourcing  
↓  
Resume Screening  
↓  
Recruiter Screening

**1. Manpower Requirement

Manpower requirement is the process of identifying the need for a new employee in an organization.

**2. Job Requisition

A job requisition is a formal request to start the hiring process for a particular position.

**3. Job Description (JD)

A Job Description explains the role and tells candidates what the organization is looking for.

**4. Candidate Sourcing

Candidate sourcing is the process of finding suitable candidates for an open position.
Recruiters can also use Boolean search to find candidates with specific skills and experience.

**5. Resume Screening

Resume screening is the process of reviewing resumes to identify candidates who match the Job Description.
The recruiter compares the candidate's profile with the must-have requirements before moving the candidate to the next stage.

**6. Recruiter Screening

Recruiter screening is an initial conversation with a shortlisted candidate.
The purpose is to confirm whether the candidate is suitable and interested before forwarding the profile to the technical/hiring team.
